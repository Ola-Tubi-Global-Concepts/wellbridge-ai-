## Seed classifications

- **Officially published:** a government, facility or programme source publishes the identity/contact/claim. This does not prove current operation.
- **Direct confirmation pending:** WellBridge AI has not received and logged direct confirmation from the responsible operator.
- **Research only:** a candidate requiring authoritative identity or operational confirmation.
