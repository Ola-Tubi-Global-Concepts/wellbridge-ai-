# Nigeria healthcare pathway verification register

**Project:** WellBridge AI  
**Pilot scope:** Nigeria, beginning with Lagos State  
**Research reviewed:** 18 November 2026  
**Register status:** Preliminary desk research; not yet approved for live referral or emergency use.

## Publication rule

A source being publicly available does not prove that a telephone line is currently answered, a service is operational, a facility is accepting patients, or a clinician is available. Do not describe any pathway as live, guaranteed, monitored, or endorsed by WellBridge AI until the responsible organisation has confirmed the details directly and that confirmation has been recorded.

Do not use this register as a clinical protocol. WellBridge AI is not an emergency response service and cannot dispatch assistance.

## 1. Emergency response

### Nigeria national emergency number — 112

- **Source:** Nigerian Communications Commission emergency services number listing: https://ncc.gov.ng/media/836/view
- **Government policy context:** National Economic Council approval announced 30 April 2026: https://statehouse.gov.ng/nec-moves-to-strengthen-national-emergency-response-okays-112-as-lifeline/
- **Implementation context:** Federal Ministry of Information and National Orientation announcement dated 8 September 2026 describes work on a nationwide operational roadmap: https://fmino.gov.ng/fg-moves-to-operationalise-112-national-emergency-number/
- **What the sources establish:** 112 is allocated in Nigeria's National Numbering Plan for emergency services; NEC approved its adoption as the national emergency number.
- **What remains unconfirmed:** Actual call handling, coverage, dispatch capability, response time and service availability in each state and locality.
- **Register status:** officially-published / operational-confirmation-pending
- **Publication rule:** Do not promise nationwide connection or response. Confirm state-level operational arrangements before presenting it as a reliable live route.

### Lagos State emergency response — 767 or 112

- **Source:** Lagos State Government website: https://www.lagosstate.gov.ng/
- **Additional Lagos State public service page:** https://citizensgate.lagosstate.gov.ng/
- **What the sources establish:** Lagos State publishes 767 and 112 as toll-free emergency lines for LASEMA.
- **What remains unconfirmed:** Current call-answering, dispatch coverage and the precise response pathway for mental-health emergencies.
- **Register status:** officially-published / operational-confirmation-pending
- **Publication rule:** Keep this distinct from a specialist mental-health helpline. Confirm the LASEMA process for medical and mental-health emergencies before describing the service response.

## 2. Mental-health crisis and psychosocial support

### Lagos Lifeline — numbers published by Lagos State

- **Numbers announced by Lagos State Government:** 0700 000 6463 and 090 9000 6463.
- **Government announcement (12 October 2024):** https://lagosstate.gov.ng/news/Health%20Services/view/6722bfa71415802f26321399
- **Service information published by Lagos MiND:** https://lagosmind.org/lagos-lifeline/
- **What the sources establish:** Lagos State announced the helpline numbers as part of its mental-health support initiative. Lagos MiND describes the Lifeline as a counselling and psychosocial support service and states that it operates 24/7.
- **Important limitation:** The 24/7 service description is published by Lagos MiND; this desk review has not independently tested either number or obtained direct current confirmation from the responsible service operator. Do not assume that a call will be answered or that a counsellor is available at all times.
- **Register status:** government-announced / operator-confirmation-and-live-test-pending
- **Required before publication:** Obtain written confirmation from Lagos State Ministry of Health or the authorised operator covering ownership, active numbers, hours, staffing/qualification, cost, languages, eligibility, confidentiality limits, escalation procedure, referral arrangements, and downtime fallback. Conduct an agreed non-emergency test call; never test by simulating a crisis.

## 3. Specialist mental-health facilities

### Federal Neuro-Psychiatric Hospital, Yaba, Lagos

- **Official hospital website:** https://fnphyaba.gov.ng/
- **Official contact numbers displayed on the hospital website:** +234 815 517 0000; +234 906 000 1907.
- **Official patient information:** https://mail.fnphyaba.gov.ng/health-information/
- **Clinic days:** https://www.fnphyaba.gov.ng/our-clinic-days/
- **Federal Ministry of Health specialty-hospital listing:** https://health.gov.ng/specialty-hospitals/
- **What the sources establish:** The hospital identifies itself as a specialist mental-health provider. Its patient information page states that its emergency unit operates 24 hours daily and that first-time patients may enter through the emergency or amenity departments. The hospital publishes outpatient clinic schedules.
- **Important limitation:** The general contact numbers are not identified as direct emergency-unit lines. Confirm the current address, entry procedure, emergency-unit access, fees, age limits, service capacity and contact routing directly with the hospital.
- **Register status:** official-facility / service-details-confirmation-pending
- **Publication rule:** It may be listed as a specialist facility candidate, not as a guaranteed referral or a substitute for emergency services.

## 4. Primary-care and general-hospital network

### Lagos primary healthcare centres and district referral facilities

- **Lagos MiND facilities directory:** https://lagosmind.org/facilities/
- **What the source states:** It describes the Mental Health in Primary Care (MeHPriC) programme, developed with Lagos State Ministry of Health support, and reports training of healthcare workers across 57 flagship primary healthcare centres. It describes PHC clusters linked to state general hospitals as district referral points and lists candidate PHCs and general hospitals.
- **Important limitation:** A directory entry or historic training report does not establish that a particular PHC currently has a trained mental-health worker, that the service is available today, or that a referral is accepted.
- **Register status:** network-candidate / facility-level-confirmation-pending
- **Required before publication:** Verify each facility's current operating status, mental-health service scope, opening hours, accessibility, fees, referral destination and named institutional contact with the Lagos State Primary Health Care Board or relevant facility management.

## 5. Practitioner credential verification

### Medical and Dental Council of Nigeria (MDCN)

- **Official website:** https://mdcn.gov.ng/
- **Doctor-status confirmation service:** https://mdcn.gov.ng/
- **What the source establishes:** MDCN provides a service to confirm the status of medical practitioners and dentists and regulates the practice of medicine and dentistry.
- **Register status:** regulator-identified / individual-check-required
- **Publication rule:** Verify each doctor individually through the regulator's current process and retain the verification date and evidence. MDCN verification must not be treated as verification of every category of mental-health professional.

### Lagos Community of Providers (LCOP)

- **Lagos MiND provider-directory page:** https://lagosmind.org/providers/
- **What the page states:** It describes the Lagos Community of Providers as a Lagos State Ministry of Health initiative and says public-facing profiles are subject to a multi-step verification process.
- **Important limitation:** The directory's description is not a substitute for checking a clinician's statutory registration with the appropriate professional regulator. Non-clinical community support roles must be clearly labelled and must not be presented as licensed clinical care.
- **Register status:** directory-initiative-identified / individual-profile-and-regulator-check-required

## 6. Required verification record for every pathway

Before a service is shown as an active option in WellBridge AI, record:

1. Country, state, locality and service category.
2. Official service/facility name and legal operator.
3. Official source URL and date accessed.
4. Direct confirmation date, method, name/role of confirming representative and evidence reference.
5. Telephone number, messaging channel, address and verified contact route.
6. Operating hours, eligibility, age limits, language, cost and accessibility.
7. Service scope and exclusions; distinguish counselling, clinical assessment, specialist treatment and emergency response.
8. Practitioner regulator and individual registration check where relevant.
9. Referral acceptance process, escalation procedure and fallback if unavailable.
10. Last verified date, next review date, owner and current status.

Suggested statuses: research-only, officially-published, direct-confirmation-pending, verified-active, temporarily-unavailable, expired, rejected.
